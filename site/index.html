A temporary dispensation has been applied to the assessment plan version ST0270/AP01/V1.0 and ST0270/V1.1 for this apprenticeship. The dispensation will run indefinitely until all apprentices registered to ST0270/AP01/V1.0 and ST0270/V1.1 only, are complete. The dispensation may be withdrawn if no longer necessary.
